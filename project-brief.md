# My Project Brief

## The Question
Which Populated Wards and Settlements in Kaltungo LGA, Gombe State have poor access to health facilities based on population distribution and the shortest available road-network to the nearest health facility?

## Why it Matters
Access to healthcare depends not only on the number and distribution of health facilities but also on how easily people can physically reach them. A health facility may appear close when measured using straight-line distance but may require a much longer journey when people travel through the available road network.
This project would help health planners, local government authorities, humanitarian organisations, and development agencies identify populated settlements with poor access to health facilities. The results could support decisions on where to conduct further field assessments, improve transportation links, establish new facilities, or prioritize mobile health services.

## The Data I Need
- Health Facility Locations
- Road and Path Network
- Population Distribution Data
- Ward Boundaries
- Kaltungo LGA Boundary

## What I will Build
I will build a system that would show the spatial distribution of population, settlements, health facilities, and the road network. It would calculate the shortest road-network distance from populated settlements to the nearest health facility and identify high-population areas with poor accessibility.
